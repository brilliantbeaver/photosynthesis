Your study plan

A week or more before

  1. Read Guide 5 (Detected Errors) and fix each item on your own sheets.
  2. Read Guide 1 (Learning Guide), one or two Parts a day. Do the Part 11 flash cards.
  3. Read Guide 2 (CER Prep), Sections 1–6.
  4. Ask classmates for the real Part IV (light color) and Part V (temperature) numbers.

2–4 days before

  1. Work through the three worked examples in Guide 2.
  2. Do Guide 3 Levels 1–2 (Q1–Q6). Score each with the rubric.
  3. Then Levels 3–4 (Q7–Q12). Redo any question scoring below 5/6.
  4. Rewrite your own Part III conclusion as a full CER.

The night before & morning of

  1. Read the Cheat Sheet (Guide 4) twice.
  2. Re-read the Top 5 fixes in Guide 5.
  3. Say the two equations out loud — with the right energy label!
  4. Sleep. A rested brain reasons better than a crammed one.
● Key idea — If you remember only three things
  1. Light energy goes IN to photosynthesis (chloroplast); ATP energy comes OUT of respiration (mitochondria). Plants do both; animals and decomposers respire.
  2. Evidence = specific numbers with units (e.g., O2 production rose from about 0 at 20–40 % light to 0.5976 mg/L per hour at 100 %).
  3. Reasoning = the science “because” that links your evidence to your claim — not a repeat of the data.
! Watch out — A note about these materials

They follow your class packets closely. Where they go beyond the packets, they say so (“Reasonable inference”). Practice data that was made up is labeled HYPOTHETICAL PRACTICE DATA. If anything here disagrees with what your teacher said in class, go with your teacher — and ask about it!
