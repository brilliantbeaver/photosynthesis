Quick self-test

Quick self-test: click a card to check yourself
1 Which energy goes IN to photosynthesis, and which comes OUT of cellular respiration?
Light energy goes in to photosynthesis (stored as chemical energy in glucose). Energy comes out of respiration as ATP (plus some heat).
2 Which living things do photosynthesis AND respiration?
Plants (they have chloroplasts and mitochondria). Animals and decomposers only respire.
3 What was the DO difference in the dark run (Part II), and what does it measure?
−0.1939 mg/L per hour: respiration alone (no light, so no photosynthesis).
4 At 100 % light, what were the DO difference and the O2 production?
DO difference +0.4037 mg/L per hour (net, what the water kept); O2 production 0.5976 mg/L per hour (gross) = 0.4037 + 0.1939.
5 Why is O2 production = DO difference + 0.1939?
The sheet says DO difference − respiration, and respiration = −0.1939. Minus a negative is a plus.
6 Near which light level did the plant break even?
Near 60 %: DO difference +0.0046 ≈ 0, so O2 made ≈ O2 used (one trial).
7 Name the three classic limiting factors of photosynthesis.
Light intensity, CO2 concentration and temperature.
8 Why does Mauna Loa CO2 peak in May and dip in Sept–Oct?
CO2 is a running total: in fall and winter, respiration + decay add more than photosynthesis removes, so it climbs until May; then summer photosynthesis pulls it down to the low in Sept–Oct.
9 Part VII: how do you find how many fish the tank can support?
Keep the DO difference ≥ 0. One plant at 100 % light leaves about 0.40 mg/L per hour for all the fish together; add fish one at a time and keep the last number with DO difference ≥ 0.
10 How are the CER points split?
Claim 1 + Evidence 2 + Reasoning 3 = 6 (your teacher’s rubric may differ slightly).
